# EDR 0020: Direction change — user-confirmed live execution with a risk gate

- **Status:** Accepted (2026-07-19) — explicit direction commit from the user, intentionally superseding parts of EDR 0017. Items marked **(open)** are scoping decisions still owed before their phase ships.
- **Scope:** product plane + key custody + a new execution plane. **No engine decision/trigger semantics change and no `ENGINE_VERSION` change** — the 2.0.0 forward-test clock keeps running untouched.
- **Supersedes:** EDR 0017 decision 1 (the "read-only forever" execution clause) and decision 4 (the reject-all-trading-keys intake gate, for the designated execution key only). EDR 0017 decisions 2 (engine stance), 3 (R-normalization), and 5 (TradFi gating) **remain in force**, as does its AI-complement stance in the amended form below.

## Problem

EDR 0017 committed Market Pulse to read-only: journal, brief, and behavior review, never execution. The 2026-07-19 direction change (the "Trading Engine v2" vision, narrowed by the user's follow-up) concludes that the behavior-review layer cannot deliver its core value — consistent risk management — as advice alone. Rules only bind when the platform is the order path. IQ will therefore support **live execution via Binance**, under the constraints below, which are the decision — not decoration on it.

## The decisions

### 1. Execution scope — user-confirmed, never autonomous

IQ places orders on Binance only when the user explicitly confirms that specific order. There is **no auto-trading**: no unattended entries, no AI-initiated orders, no conditional automation that fires without a confirmation. On an approved, confirmed trade IQ automatically:

- calculates position size from the configured per-trade risk (0.5–3% band) — the user never enters a raw quantity;
- places the order with a **mandatory stop-loss attached immediately** (an order without a stop cannot be submitted);
- attaches take-profit or strategy-defined exits;
- creates the trade journal entry.

Post-entry modifications follow the trade-lock principle: risk on an open trade may be reduced (trail, break-even, partial TP) but not increased through IQ — no stop removal, no leverage increase, no averaging down.

### 2. The risk gate — deterministic rules bind; AI narrates and advises

Every order passes a pre-trade gate before the confirm button is even live. Two layers, deliberately separated:

- **The Trading Constitution is deterministic server-side code**, not LLM judgment: risk-per-trade band, daily/weekly loss limits, max leverage, max concurrent positions, max portfolio exposure, minimum R:R, mandatory stop. A hard-limit violation is a binding rejection with a stated reason. **No AI output can override a deterministic rejection**, and no UI path bypasses the gate.
- **The AI acts as Chief Risk Officer on top**: it explains rejections in context, and advises on judgment calls the rules can't settle mechanically — regime suitability, volatility vs. stop distance, revenge/overtrade/tilt patterns read from the user's own journal. Its outcomes are approve / recommend-reduce / recommend-wait / reject-with-explanation. Consistent with EDR 0017, the AI **still never originates signals** — it gates and cross-examines the user's decision; it does not propose entries.

### 3. Honest enforcement claim

IQ cannot restrict the user's exchange account itself; nothing prevents trading directly on Binance around the platform. The claim made in the UI and docs is exactly: **"every trade placed through IQ is constitution-checked"** — not account-level lockout. Trades executed outside IQ still enter the journal via sync and count against behavioral analytics, which is where going around the gate becomes visible.

### 4. Custody — a designated execution key, still withdrawal-rejected

The intake gate flips from "reject any trading-enabled key" to a two-class model:

- **Read-only sync keys**: unchanged (EDR 0017 decision 4 model — AES-256-GCM at rest, permission-checked at intake).
- **One designated execution key per account**: trade scope accepted; **withdrawal scope is still rejected outright at intake**, permanently. Same encryption-at-rest model, stored and handled as a separate class from sync keys; exchange-side IP allowlisting to this server is required at intake, not recommended.

### 5. Trade Quality Score — deterministic evaluation, not prediction

Execution surfaces show no "AI Confidence". In its place: a **Trade Quality
Score** (0–100) generated by the deterministic engine layer from a published
rubric — R:R, stop-placement validity, constitution headroom,
volatility-vs-stop-distance, session/liquidity context, behavior flags — with
the per-component breakdown displayable. It is an **evaluation of
rule-compliance and setup quality, not a win-probability**, and is labeled as
such at every render site (M0 score-honesty rules apply). The AI may explain
the score; it never generates, adjusts, or re-weights it.

### 6. The Trade Permit — no order without one

Every execution attempt produces a persisted, immutable **Trade Permit**: a
snapshot of the proposal (symbol, side, entry, stop, targets, risk %), the
account state it was judged against, every deterministic check's result, the
Trade Quality Score with components, and the decision — `APPROVED` or
`REJECTED` with enumerated reasons (e.g. daily risk exhausted, R:R below
minimum, excessive correlated exposure). Permits carry a short TTL (market
state moves; a stale permit cannot be executed). The confirm control only
goes live on an approved, unexpired permit; the placed order and its journal
entry reference the permit id. **Rejected permits are persisted too** — the
record of what the desk refused is behavioral data.

### 7. Rollout gates

- **Global kill switch**: execution code ships behind an environment flag (default **off**); flipping it on is a deliberate operator action, and turning it off instantly reverts IQ to read-only.
- **Testnet first**: the execution path is built and exercised against Binance testnet (the "paper trading" future-feature, promoted to a prerequisite) before any mainnet order is placeable.
- **(open) Infrastructure isolation**: this VPS is simultaneously the dev tree and production, with push-to-main rebuild-and-restart. A trade-enabled key on this box as currently operated is the largest risk this EDR accepts *provisionally* — before the mainnet phase, an isolation decision (separate execution service/host, or hardened separation on this box) must be made and recorded here.
- **Venue (resolved 2026-07-19)**: execution is **Binance-first** — the user reaffirmed Binance in the risk-desk direction — built behind an exchange-adapter seam so the existing Bybit plumbing can join later. Bybit remains journal-sync-side only.

## What this does **not** change

- **Engine stance (0017 decision 2)**: the engine remains a context instrument pending its 2.0.0 verdict (clock reset 2026-07-17, EDR 0018). Engine reads are not presented as proven edge, and the risk gate does not treat engine verdicts as edge — it gates risk, not signal quality.
- **R-normalization (0017 decision 3)**: unchanged — and strengthened in practice, since every IQ-executed trade has an evidenced stop by construction, making R displayable for this cohort without synthesis.
- **Evidence discipline**: no backtest-derived win-rate/expectancy numbers return to the UI via this direction change; the strategy-builder/backtesting portion of the v2 vision is **out of scope for this EDR** and needs its own decision record against the M0 score-honesty rules if pursued.
- **AI custody split**: analyst BYOK keys stay in the browser; the risk gate's deterministic layer runs server-side against persisted data.

## What was intentionally rejected

- **Auto-trading in any form** — the user confirms every order; consistency, not trade count, is the goal.
- **AI-overridable limits** — an LLM approving a trade past a hard limit reintroduces the emotional bypass the constitution exists to prevent, with extra steps.
- **"Cannot be bypassed" framing** — overclaims what an exchange API key can enforce; replaced by the honest per-path claim (decision 3).
- **Synthesized position sizing inputs** — sizing derives only from account balance, stop distance, and configured risk; no volatility-guessed stops.
