## 1. The lens

Market Pulse is a **trading decision support system**, not a market data
dashboard. Target users: beginners learning consistency, and experienced
traders who overtrade, revenge-trade, FOMO, or abandon their plans. Every
feature is judged by one test:

> Does it change a trading decision — trade/skip, size, stop, exit, or the
> next day's behavior — or does it just inform?

Informational-only surfaces are cost: they add cognitive load, dilute the
verdict, and push users toward the exact overtrading the product exists to
prevent. The audit below applies that test to every milestone and every
shipped layer, with no credit for already existing.

## 2. Where the product actually is (audit)

The original M0–M8 plan assumed a linear build. Reality diverged — the
Python migration (2026-07-17), the execution direction change (EDR 0020),
and the START-HERE moves delivered a different, partly better, partly
overlapping stack:

**Built and serving the decision loop today:**
- Per-objective verdict engine (scalp/intraday/swing; not-yet /
  wrong-strategy / what-flips-it) + hysteresis — the token page headline.
- Verdict-first home (regime call in words, top setups, trades/behavior
  strip, catalyst rail) — committed 2026-07-23.
- Forward-test record 2.0.0 (Python arq worker, provenance-stamped) — the
  honesty backbone.
- Execution plane Phase A (Trading Constitution, pure risk engine, sizing,
  Trade Quality Score, immutable Trade Permits) + testnet order path with
  mandatory SL/TP — EDR 0020 core, verified on testnet.
- Binance trade review (own read-only key, sync, RR/best-worst/best-hour/
  style-fit analytics, on-demand AI verdict) — a de-facto slim M1+M3.
- Event/context ETL: news RSS (crypto + TradFi), econ calendar
  (ForexFactory), DeFiLlama unlocks, breadth; chart event overlay; Catalyst
  Impact Score 1.0.0 read plane (committed, not yet wired into verdicts).
- BYOK AI layer (analyst, desk review, econ context) — narrates, never
  originates.

**Built but informational (fails the lens test as-is):**
- Rankings / rotation / technical / regime detail panels (now tabs under
  /markets — right move, still four screens of context without a call).
- News page as a skimmable feed.
- Market-opportunities discovery scan (all-pairs), spike detector — raw
  "look at this" surfaces with no verdict attached.
- Funding Watch (already demoted to caution-only — correct end state).

**Planned but unbuilt:** M2 context stamping, M4 memo/brief harness, M5
cohort analytics, M6 skip check, M7 TradFi mode, M8 productization, M9
Phases B–E remainder (account state, trade lock, behavior detectors, CRO,
mainnet gate).

**The structural insight:** the original plan's spine (M1→M2→M3→M5→M6)
gated the skip check — the product's thesis — behind cohort statistics
that need n≥30 per segment of the user's own trades. A beginner or a
single-owner deployment takes months to earn that n. Meanwhile EDR 0020
built a **deterministic risk desk that can already say no** without any
statistics: constitution limits, R:R, exposure, staleness, behavior flags,
catalysts. The skip check should ride that spine, not the statistics
spine. That one re-anchoring reorders almost everything below.

## 3. Milestone review

Classifications: **Keep / Redesign / Merge / Defer / Remove.**

### M0 — Honesty pass & direction commit → **Keep (close it out)**
- **Why:** honesty framing is the trust foundation for a decision product;
  it's 90% done and already paid for. Two tasks remain: M0-T6 (deploy
  path — the GH workflow still silently pretends) and M0-T7/U18 (bind
  :3002 to loopback; TLS bypass).
- **Impact:** T7 is no longer hygiene — a live futures-scoped execution
  key sits on this box (U22). Transport hardening is now execution-plane
  security.
- **Dependencies:** none. **Fit:** direct — trust and safety.
- **Disposition:** fold the two remaining tasks into R0 (ops & safety)
  below; retire the milestone file as complete.

### M1 — Read-only Binance trade ingestion → **Merge (into the journal, slim spec)**
- **Why:** its goal is now served by two shipped planes: `binance_review`
  sync (read-only key, fills, analytics) and M9's execution journal (every
  IQ-placed trade lands with permit + evidenced stop by construction).
  Building M1's full 12-task spec (position reconstruction, 0.1%
  reconciliation harness, resumable backfill) duplicates that with heavy
  rigor a single-user product doesn't need yet.
- **What survives:** order-history sync for **stop evidence** (M1-T6) —
  it's the R-rule input for trades placed outside IQ, and the only piece
  neither shipped plane covers. Also the auth-gating criterion (journal
  data never public).
- **Impact:** unlocks honest R and stop-discipline forensics for external
  trades without rebuilding what exists.
- **Dependencies:** none (review sync already runs).
- **Fit:** partial as specced; full as merged. **Disposition:** merge the
  stop-evidence task into R4; drop the reconciliation harness and full
  reconstruction until multi-user demands it.

### M2 — Context stamping (engine replay) → **Redesign (stamp-at-open only; defer replay)**
- **Why:** the payoff (per-trade "what did the market look like") is real
  and feeds behavior review. But 6 of 8 tasks serve **historical replay
  backfill** — expensive, fragile (bar-limited replay, determinism
  proofs), and its main consumer was M5's cohort stats, which this plan
  defers. M2-T8 (stamp new trades at open, live context, no replay) is
  cheap and delivers most of the user value: every trade from now on
  carries its context.
- **Impact:** every reviewed trade can say "you entered counter-regime
  during a high-impact event window" — the concrete input behavior change
  needs.
- **Dependencies:** review sync (exists), catalyst store (exists).
- **Fit:** direct in redesigned form. **Disposition:** stamp-at-open +
  context card ship in R4; replay backfill deferred with M5.

### M3 — Per-trade behavior forensics → **Keep (redesigned scope, high priority)**
- **Why:** this is the "review objectively / improve behavior" core, built
  on single-trade facts — no statistics needed, honest at n=1. Partially
  shipped (RR, best/worst, best-hour, style-fit). Missing pieces are
  exactly what the target user needs: MAE/MFE, exit efficiency, **stop
  discipline** (widened/removed stops), **re-entry latency after loss**
  (the revenge tell), sizing variance.
- **Impact:** highest of any analytics work — it names the specific
  behaviors (revenge, FOMO, stop abandonment) the product promises to fix,
  per trade, with evidence.
- **Dependencies:** stop evidence (merged M1-T6), stamp-at-open (M2
  redesign) for the context-flavored metrics; excursions need only klines.
- **Fit:** direct. **Disposition:** R4.

### M4 — AI complement layer → **Merge (into CRO + review; drop the standalone brief)**
- **Why:** two products hid in M4. (a) The per-trade memo — valuable,
  merges into R4's trade review (narrate the forensics facts). (b) The
  daily brief — **superseded by the verdict-first home**: the home *is*
  the daily brief, deterministic and glanceable; a second AI-written
  morning artifact adds reading load, not decision quality. The
  groundedness harness survives as a slim check (numeric claims must
  appear in the prompt payload) applied to memo + CRO output — the full
  CI-fixture program is overweight for BYOK surfaces.
- **Impact:** keeps AI in its lane (narrator), avoids a daily-content
  treadmill.
- **Dependencies:** forensics (R4), permits (exist).
- **Fit:** partial. **Disposition:** memo + slim harness into R4; CRO
  narration into R5; standalone scheduled brief **removed**.

### M5 — Cohort analytics (pre-registered) → **Defer (until the journal earns n)**
- **Why:** statistically the most honest milestone in the plan — and
  precisely because it's honest, it renders "insufficient evidence" for
  months at realistic trade rates. Its pre-registration discipline is
  right and is preserved as the bar for *when* it returns. Deterministic
  per-trade forensics + behavior detectors deliver the actionable subset
  now without fake precision.
- **Impact of deferring:** near zero today; the data (journal + stamps)
  keeps accumulating regardless, so nothing is lost — the clock runs
  while we build elsewhere.
- **Re-entry trigger:** ≥30 closed, stamped trades in the segment family's
  largest buckets (check quarterly; protocol written *before* any UI, as
  originally specced).
- **Fit:** direct but premature. **Disposition:** deferred, trigger-gated.

### M6 — Pre-trade skip check → **Keep (redesigned spine — this is the product)**
- **Why:** the thesis feature — most tools push trades; this one protects
  the user from themselves. **Redesign:** the original gated it on M5
  cohort stats. Re-anchor it on what's already built and deterministic:
  a **dry-run Trade Permit** (risk engine, constitution, sizing, exposure,
  TQS — no order intent required) + regime verdict + catalyst impact
  window + deterministic behavior flags (M9-T11). Cohort claims join later
  when M5 un-defers; until then those blocks render "no opinion —
  insufficient evidence", which the original spec already treats as a
  feature, not a gap.
- **Impact:** directly serves goals 1–3 (trade/skip, what matters, risk
  before the trade); it's the retention hook and the differentiator.
- **Dependencies:** M9 Phase A (done), account-state service (M9-T7),
  catalyst wiring (R1), behavior detectors (R3).
- **Fit:** the center of it. **Disposition:** R2, immediately after
  catalyst wiring.

### M7 — TradFi mode → **Remove (as a mode); keep macro as context input**
- **Why:** a second asset-class mode is breadth, not decision quality —
  nothing in it helps the target user skip a bad trade or size correctly.
  It carries the plan's worst risk/effort profile: instrument-class
  abstraction, session-gap-aware settlement walkers, applicability audits
  across every engine read — all to serve demand that is asserted, not
  evidenced. The valuable kernel already shipped by another route: the
  econ calendar + TradFi news ETL feed crypto decisions as **catalyst
  context** (CPI/FOMC windows gate crypto verdicts too).
- **Impact of removing:** frees the largest single block of planned effort
  (10 tasks) at no cost to the seven user goals.
- **Re-entry condition:** a real user cohort trading Binance TradFi
  tickers asks for it — then it starts, as before, with the T1 instrument
  survey and its own EDR.
- **Disposition:** removed from the active roadmap; macro-as-catalyst
  stays (already live).

### M8 — Productization → **Defer (split out the ops-critical subset)**
- **Why:** onboarding, multi-user isolation suites, sync budgets for N
  users, landing pages — all premature before the decision loop is proven
  on its one real user. But three tasks are not productization, they're
  **operational safety for a box holding a live trading key**: deploy
  path (M8-T5/M0-T6), backup/restore drill (M8-T6), alerting completeness
  (M8-T7).
- **Disposition:** those three merge into R0; the rest defers behind a
  trigger — the owner completes the full loop (verdict → skip-check →
  permit → trade → review) for 4+ consecutive weeks and wants a second
  user. Multi-user schema discipline (user_id FKs from day one) stays a
  standing guardrail so the deferral stays cheap.

### M9 — Execution plane → **Keep (top priority to finish; the risk spine)**
- **Why:** EDR 0020's reasoning is the product's reasoning: risk rules
  only bind when the platform is the order path. The constitution +
  permit + trade-lock stack is *the* risk-management deliverable
  (goal 3), and its refusal record is behavioral data (goal 6). Phase A
  is done and testnet round-trip verified; what remains maps directly to
  user outcomes: T7 account state (fail-closed staleness), T10 trade lock
  (no stop-widening, no averaging down — discipline enforced mid-trade),
  T11 deterministic behavior detectors (revenge/overtrade/tilt →
  advisory flags or binding cooldowns), T12 AI CRO narration.
- **Impact:** converts every risk-management promise from advice to
  enforcement on the IQ path.
- **Dependencies:** U23/U24 (kill-switch custody, infra isolation) gate
  mainnet only — everything else proceeds on testnet.
- **Fit:** direct. **Disposition:** R3 (build) + R6 (mainnet gate,
  owner-decision-bound).

### Shipped non-milestone layers

- **Verdict-first home + /markets tabs** — **Keep.** The correct spine;
  finish by wiring impact scores in (R1).
- **Catalyst Impact Score 1.0.0** — **Keep.** Committed, unconsumed;
  wiring it into verdicts is the highest-leverage small task in the repo
  (R1).
- **Market-opportunities discovery scan** — **Redesign.** As an
  all-pairs "opportunities" wall it invites FOMO — the anti-goal. Rebuild
  as the **alternatives answer** (goal 4): only rendered when the user's
  chosen setup is invalid, only tokens with a currently-valid
  per-objective verdict, ranked, capped at 3 ("your setup isn't there;
  these are"). R5.
- **Spike detector** — **Redesign (invert the frame).** A spike alert
  reads as "jump in"; for this user it should read "do not chase".
  Alerts and scanner badges get an explicit don't-chase framing +
  post-spike cooldown note. Small copy/logic task, folded into R5.
- **Rankings / rotation / technical tabs** — **Keep (demoted).** Already
  one route; they serve "understand what matters" for the minority who
  drill down. No further investment; candidates for pruning if usage
  stays low. Regime tab stays — it feeds the home verdict.
- **News page** — **Merge.** The feed adds skim-load; impact-gated alerts
  + catalyst rail + verdict integration replace it. Keep the route as a
  filtered archive reachable from catalyst items; remove it from primary
  nav (part of R1's noise-gating).
- **Funding Watch (caution-only)** — **Keep as-is.** Already correctly
  demoted; it's risk context now.
- **Forward-test record surfaces** — **Keep.** Cheap, running, and the
  honesty proof behind every engine read. No change.
- **Tracker / trades / review routes** — **Keep**, converging into the
  single journal plane as R4 lands.

## 4. Revised roadmap

Ordered by user impact per unit of remaining work. Each item is
independently shippable; none touches engine semantics.

### R0 — Safety & ops floor *(do first, small)*
- **Objective:** the box that holds a live trading key stops being the
  weakest link; one true deploy path.
- **User problem:** none directly — this protects the user's capital and
  the product's existence. A leaked execution key or a broken prod tree
  outranks every feature.
- **Deliverables:** M0-T7 loopback bind + U18 firewall (owner); M0-T6
  deploy path resolved (fix CI or bless the manual script); backup/restore
  drill (M8-T6); alerting sweep (M8-T7); restart both services to pick up
  the committed home/tabs/impact work (owner, U4).
- **Impact:** removes the standing TLS bypass with a trade-scoped key
  present; makes every later ship safe.
- **Dependencies:** owner actions U18/U4. **Order:** first — it's small
  and everything rides on it.

### R1 — Catalysts into the verdict *(the pending START-HERE step)*
- **Objective:** events stop being a feed; they modify the call.
- **User problem:** "what information actually matters" (goal 2) — users
  can't weigh an unlock or CPI print against a setup; the product now
  does it for them.
- **Deliverables:** impact/direction wired into the home catalyst rail
  and the token verdict card ("swing valid — but 4.1% unlock in 32h:
  size down / wait"); catalyst as an explicit what-flips-it condition;
  alerts gated on impact ≥ threshold (kills roundup noise); news page out
  of primary nav.
- **Impact:** immediate, visible decision-quality lift on the two screens
  users already open; reduces noise instead of adding a surface.
- **Dependencies:** Impact Score plane (committed), R0 restarts.
- **Order:** second — smallest work, largest visible payoff.

### R2 — Skip Check v1 *(the thesis feature, re-anchored)*
- **Objective:** before risking capital, the user gets a deterministic
  answer: supportive read / stated cautions / "no opinion — insufficient
  evidence", plus what-would-change-it.
- **User problem:** goals 1 and 3 head-on — trade or skip, risk before
  the trade. This is the anti-overtrading, anti-FOMO surface.
- **Deliverables:** typed response contract (no free-text claim path);
  dry-run permit (risk engine + constitution + sizing headroom, no order);
  regime/objective verdict block; catalyst-window block (from R1);
  behavior-flag block (advisory until R3 detectors land, then live);
  entry points on token page + home; invocation log with later-trade
  linkage (the product's own effectiveness record); ≤3s answer budget.
- **Impact:** the differentiator; converts the whole stack into one
  question answered honestly. The invocation log seeds future evidence
  that skip-check answers correlate with outcomes.
- **Dependencies:** M9 Phase A (done), R1; M9-T7 account state for the
  exposure block (can ship stubbed as "no opinion" if R3 lags).
- **Order:** third.

### R3 — Execution plane completion (testnet) 
- **Objective:** finish EDR 0020's desk: T7 account state (fail-closed),
  T10 trade lock (reduce-only management: trail/BE/partial TP; no stop
  removal or widening, no averaging down), T11 deterministic behavior
  detectors (revenge/overtrade/tilt → permit flags; constitution can opt
  into binding cooldowns).
- **User problem:** goals 3 and 6 — risk managed during the trade, not
  just before; discipline enforced where advice fails.
- **Deliverables:** the three task groups above, each with the M9 DoD
  tests (fail-closed, forbidden-action negatives, seeded detector
  fixtures); detectors also feed R2's behavior block.
- **Impact:** risk rules become enforcement on every IQ-path trade;
  refusals persist as behavioral data.
- **Dependencies:** none external until mainnet (all testnet);
  kill-switch stays off. **Order:** fourth, parallel-capable with R4.

### R4 — Trade review that changes behavior
- **Objective:** every closed trade tells the user what they did, in
  facts, and the review names the habit.
- **User problem:** goals 5 and 6 — objective review, behavior
  improvement.
- **Deliverables:** stop-evidence order sync (slim M1-T6); stamp-at-open
  context (slim M2: regime, session, verdict-at-open, active catalysts —
  live context, no replay); forensics set (MAE/MFE, exit efficiency, stop
  discipline, re-entry latency, sizing variance) with
  `docs/forensics-definitions.md` first; R only where a stop is evidenced
  (EDR 0017 rule, test-asserted); per-trade AI memo grounded in those
  rows with the slim groundedness check; distributions view (counts and
  histograms only, no edge claims).
- **Impact:** the improvement loop closes — trade → facts → named habit →
  next skip-check remembers it (via R3 detectors reading the same rows).
- **Dependencies:** review sync (live); R3 detectors benefit but don't
  block. **Order:** fifth, interleavable with R3.

### R5 — Alternatives & guardrail surfaces
- **Objective:** when the preferred setup is invalid, answer "then what?"
  without feeding FOMO.
- **User problem:** goal 4 — and the overtrading failure mode where an
  invalid setup gets forced because nothing else is offered.
- **Deliverables:** discovery scan rebuilt as verdict-gated alternatives
  (max 3, each with objective + what-flips-it, shown only from a
  skip/invalid state); spike surfaces reframed as don't-chase warnings
  with cooldown notes; AI CRO narration (M9-T12) over permits and
  skip-checks — explains, never decides, schema-proven.
- **Impact:** patience becomes a navigable path instead of a dead end;
  the AI voice arrives only after the deterministic layer it narrates is
  complete.
- **Dependencies:** R2 (skip states to hang alternatives on), R3
  (permits/detectors for CRO). **Order:** sixth.

### R6 — Mainnet gate *(owner-decision-bound)*
- **Objective:** M9-T13/T14 — infra-isolation decision (U24) recorded in
  EDR 0020, mainnet checklist, soft launch with minimum sizes and a daily
  permit↔journal↔exchange reconciliation window.
- **User problem:** goal 7 — real account growth under the desk's rules.
- **Impact:** the loop runs on real capital with every guardrail proven.
- **Dependencies:** R0 (hardening), R3 (complete desk), owner U23/U24.
- **Order:** whenever the owner records U24 — the roadmap never blocks
  on it.

### Deferred (trigger-gated, not deleted)
- **M5 cohort analytics** — trigger: n≥30 in major segments; protocol
  frozen before any UI.
- **M2 historical replay backfill** — trigger: M5 un-defers (it's the
  main consumer).
- **M8 productization remainder** (onboarding, multi-user suites, sync
  budgets, landing) — trigger: owner runs the full loop 4+ weeks and
  recruits a second user (U15).

### Removed
- **M7 TradFi mode** — breadth without decision value for the target
  user; macro calendar already serves crypto decisions as context.
  Re-entry needs evidenced demand + its own EDR.
- **M4 standalone scheduled daily brief** — superseded by the
  verdict-first home; a second morning artifact is reading load.
- **M1 full reconstruction + 0.1% reconciliation harness** — rigor
  without a consumer at current scale.
- **All-pairs "opportunities" wall & jump-in spike framing** — replaced
  by R5's gated alternatives and don't-chase framing.
